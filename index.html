Research & Applied AI

Projects that became published papers

Each of these started as a signal-processing problem on real data - a heartbeat, a bearing, a camera feed - and ended up peer-reviewed.

Biomedical Signal Processing · ECG

Edge-Efficient Autoencoder Framework for ECG Arrhythmia Detection

A lightweight unsupervised anomaly-detection framework for real-time arrhythmia detection on edge and wearable devices, no labelled pathological data required. Evaluated Flat VAE and LSTM-VAE architectures, then extended into a shallow Conv1D autoencoder with threshold-based anomaly detection for edge deployment.

🏆 Best Paper Award - IEEE CCPIS 2025 (co-authored with Dr. D. Ganga)
Read the paper (DOI) ↗
Mechanical Diagnostics · Vibration Analysis

AI-Based Bearing Fault Diagnosis Using Vibration Signals

Bearing fault-diagnosis frameworks for electrical machines, progressing from classical ML and VAE-based approaches to 1D-CNN architectures with latent-space anomaly scoring. Includes class-conditional trust and anomaly-scoring methods for confidence quantification and selective classification under uncertain operating conditions.

📄 Published - VETOMAC 2025, IIT Guwahati (Springer Proceedings) · 2 papers
Computer Vision · Construction Safety

PPE Compliance Detection for Construction Sites - "Eagle Eyes"

A YOLO-based computer-vision system monitoring PPE compliance (helmet, vest) on construction sites in real time. Exported and benchmarked across ONNX, TensorFlow Lite, and TensorFlow.js for CPU, edge / mobile, and browser-based deployment respectively.

📄 Published as a Springer LNCS book chapter - PREMI 2025, IIT Delhi
